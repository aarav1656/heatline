Schema corrections found by inspecting inputSchema live (not guessed):
  match_condition_to_code takes {text: string}, not {description: string}.
  request_evidence takes {ask: string}.
  assemble_hp_action_packet and file_packet take {} (no args).
  draft_311_complaint (declarative form) takes {conditionType, description}; calling it
    fills the form and shows "An agent filled this in. Read it, then press the button
    yourself." — no toolautosubmit, a human clicks Send. The confirm-promise itself
    rejects with "Tool execution cancelled by a form reset" after the human submits,
    which is expected UI behavior, not a failure (the fields fill correctly either way).
